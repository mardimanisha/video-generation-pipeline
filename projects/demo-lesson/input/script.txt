Welcome to today's lesson. We are going to talk about compound interest, one of the most powerful ideas in personal finance.

What exactly is compound interest? Simply put, it is interest that you earn on your interest. Your money grows, and then that growth starts growing too.

Let's look at a simple example. Imagine you invest one thousand dollars at an annual return of eight percent. After the first year, you have one thousand and eighty dollars.

In the second year, you earn eight percent on the full amount, not just on your original deposit. That small difference, repeated year after year, is what makes compounding so remarkable.

There are three things that matter most. First, the rate of return. Second, the amount you invest. Third, and most important of all, time.

Remember, the earlier you start, the more time your money has to work for you. Thank you for watching, and I will see you in the next lesson.
